Camp Kesem

August 24, 2025 · 1 min read
#memory

This summer, I had the pleasure of being a counselor for the olden teen group for Camp Kesem, a sleepaway camp for kids whose parents have or had cancer. It's funny because even though I was supposed to be a role model and emotional supporter for the kids, they more than served that role for me, whether by inviting me into big group hugs, offering a shoulder to lean on during empowerment, participating in my Moon games, laughing at my terribly corny jokes, saving up pies for me, and so much more. Those kids were my heroes, and seeing them battle through so much adversity, whether physical, financial, or emotional, and continue to try their best and support their peers through migraines and crutches and familial turmoil inspires me to try my best to create a space where everyone feels safe to be themselves and find hope.
